## Package Content Summary
- `time_series_60min_singleindex.csv`: 300 columns, 50,401 hourly rows spanning `2014-12-31 23:00:00+00:00` to `2020-09-30 23:00:00+00:00` (UTC).
- Geographical Scope: 32 European countries and bidding zones.
- Temporal Resolution: Hourly (60-minute), quarter-hourly (15-min), and half-hourly (30-min).

## Selected Geographic Bidding Zone & Target Series
- **Selected Zone**: `DK_1` (Denmark West / Jutland & Funen).
- **Justification**:
  1. Contains unbroken, contiguous data spanning 5.75 years (January 1, 2015 to September 30, 2020) without regulatory bidding zone splits.
  2. Features **100.0% valid timestamps** with strict 1-hour increments.
  3. Includes both actual generation (`DK_1_solar_generation_actual`, `DK_1_wind_generation_actual`), actual load (`DK_1_load_actual_entsoe_transparency`), official day-ahead load forecast (`DK_1_load_forecast_entsoe_transparency`), and day-ahead electricity spot prices (`DK_1_price_day_ahead` in EUR/MWh).
  4. Missing values account for less than 0.03% across all selected series and are forward-filled or flagged in preprocessing without synthetic lookahead.

## Nature of Observations
- **Actual Observations**: `solar_generation_mw`, `wind_generation_mw`, `load_actual_mw`, `price_day_ahead_eur_mwh`.
- **Source-Published Forecasts**: `load_forecast_mw` (published day-ahead by ENTSO-E). No source-published solar forecast is provided in this release; solar forecasts are generated by our models and baselines.
- **Simulated & Assumed Parameters**:
  - Commercial facility load scale: scaled to 5.0 MW peak.
  - On-site solar installation: scaled to 5.0 MW peak capacity.
  - Battery specifications: 10.0 MWh energy capacity, 2.5 MW charge/discharge power, 88% round-trip efficiency, 10%–90% SoC limits.
  - Tariffs: Day-ahead spot price + 20.0 EUR/MWh retail grid adder for import; 100% day-ahead spot price for export.
